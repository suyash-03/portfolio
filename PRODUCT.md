# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Engineering hiring teams evaluating Suyash Singh's software engineering experience and projects.

## Product Purpose

Present Suyash's backend and distributed systems work clearly, with enough concrete evidence for a hiring team to understand his role, technical depth, and outcomes, then contact him or view his résumé.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan work experience, selected projects, skills, education, and an interactive simulation of the Summit test orchestration platform.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing React site has section navigation, project category switching, a Summit simulation, a contact form that opens an email draft, social links, and a résumé link. Preserve these functions.
- Lead with backend and distributed systems work, as the user requested.
- Keep professional claims grounded in the existing portfolio content.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is Suyash Singh.
- The user wants a professional portfolio with cool, calm animations and gave the Straightforward Brand Guidelines Figma Site as a style reference.

## Evidence on Hand

- Work history and skills in `src/data/portfolioData.js`.
- Project descriptions in `src/components/Projects.js`.
- Portrait assets in `src/profile.png` and `src/about_photo.jpeg`.
- Existing Summit simulation in `src/components/SummitSimulator.js`.
- Current 2026 résumé supplied by the user for portfolio wording and dates; the live résumé action points to the user-provided Google Drive URL.

## Product Principles

- Put substantial engineering work before decorative biography.
- Make outcomes and technical decisions easy to scan.
- Let the interactive demonstration support, rather than obscure, the experience.
